---
name: Classifica Militares product promise
description: Project-level audience and trust constraint for future app work.
---

Classifica Militares is a free, public, independent platform for Brazilian military entrance exams, not an official Armed Forces service. EPCAR and AFA are the only enabled competition options in the current version; other institutions are future expansion.

**Why:** the user clarified that the product is not FAB-specific and explicitly limited current availability to EPCAR and AFA.

**How to apply:** keep the platform's language institution-neutral, identify future competitions as unavailable until supported, and keep its non-official status clear.
